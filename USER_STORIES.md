# Countersign — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Licensed professional

- As a licensed professional, I want to see what the machine produced, what it relied on and where it is uncertain, so that my countersignature is a judgment rather than an act of faith.
- As a licensed professional, I want my amendments and overrides to be recorded as a quality signal on the decision type, so that a recurring model weakness is fixed instead of being absorbed by whoever is on shift.
- As a licensed professional, I want the volume of decisions routed to me for countersignature to respect a load limit, so that I am not placed in a position where adequate supervision is arithmetically impossible.
- As a licensed professional, I want assurance that a machine-producibility score attached to my decision type cannot be used to assess me personally, so that participating in the register is not evidence against me.

### Supervising consultant or engagement partner

- As a supervising consultant, I want to see the dwell-time and override pattern for each signatory in my service, presented as supervision quality rather than as productivity, so that I can intervene where review has become nominal.
- As an engagement partner, I want independent re-review sampling to run automatically against countersigned decisions, so that calibration is measured rather than assumed.
- As a supervising consultant, I want the rostered supervision capacity for next period tested against forecast decision volume, so that I can refuse a volume increase that I cannot lawfully supervise.

### Quality and clinical governance officer

- As a governance officer, I want automated release suspended automatically when a decision type breaches its countersignature or accuracy thresholds, so that the control does not depend on somebody noticing.
- As a governance officer, I want, for any individual decision, the model version, guideline version, signatory and disclosure made, so that an incident investigation or regulatory enquiry can be answered from the record.
- As a governance officer, I want the register to hold the licensure and supervision rule for each decision type, so that a change in professional standards can be applied as a configuration change with an effective date.

### Training programme director

- As a training programme director, I want a reserved quota of training-grade decisions per trainee per rotation that automation cannot consume, so that trainees still qualify.
- As a training programme director, I want a warning when automation throughput is on course to breach the training quota, so that I can negotiate before the rotation is ruined rather than after.
- As a training programme director, I want the exposure assessment translated into curriculum gaps I can take to our university and professional body partners, so the pipeline adapts to the work that will actually remain.

### Risk officer and indemnity underwriter

- As a risk officer, I want liability exposure reported by decision type and degree of machine assistance, so that I can price and disclose our risk rather than estimate it.
- As an indemnity underwriter, I want attestable evidence that supervision is real — countersignature quality, re-review calibration, suspension history — so that I can differentiate this organisation from one with the same specialty mix and worse controls.
- As a risk officer, I want the fallback capacity for every suspendable decision type maintained and reported, so that suspending automation is a decision we can actually take.
